# Reference anchor — `taste-frontend` variant

## Anchor: Base Design / MoMA identity books — editorial sans-first voice

- Sans-serif body (Work Sans or IBM Plex Sans at 10–11pt with generous leading — 15–16pt)
- A **sans-only** book. No serifs anywhere. The discipline is about making sans-serif body prose actually readable at book length.
- Very restrained use of color — one accent (ochre, oxblood, or a muted cyan), applied to section-opening numerals or a single keyline on the cover
- Generous top margins on chapter openers (chapter title sits ~4in from top)
- Captions in a smaller size of the same sans, tracked +40 units, all-caps
- Grid is felt, not seen — no rules, no boxes, just consistent alignment and spacing

## Your job

Prove a sans-serif career report can feel editorial, not clinical. Work Sans or IBM Plex for both body and display, distinguished by size + weight only. One ochre accent. Long vertical white space above chapter titles.

## IPP treatment

Type-only composition. Center set in the sans at display size (60–72pt), satellites at body size (10pt), arrows as 0.5pt lines with no arrowheads (direction implied by proximity + a tiny indicator glyph like ‹). Everything on a 13pt baseline grid.

## Numbered markers

Small-caps lining numerals set at body size, inline, in the variant's accent color only. The color is the emphasis; no bold, no italic.

## Ban list

- No serif typography anywhere
- No boxes or rules (except hair-thin 0.25pt where structurally required)
- No centered text
- No default dark mode — warm light neutral (0.98 L)
