# Reference anchor — `huashu` variant

## Anchor: A-G Projects / Laurenz Brunner — Roma Publications aesthetic

- Structural minimalism — everything you can remove, you remove
- Sans-serif (IBM Plex Sans or Inter) at generous leading
- Page as grid — visible structural decisions, sometimes an exposed baseline grid as a design element
- Large negative space — chapter openers may run 60–80% whitespace
- Reductive IPP treatment — satellites as single words floating in the margin, center as a single typographic anchor
- Restrained color — likely black + one warm tint (bone, cream, muted red-ochre)
- Type-driven — no decorative elements

## Your job

Produce a minimal book that feels architectural. Huge whitespace. Small, confident type. Grid exposed where structural, invisible where decorative.

## IPP treatment

Single-weight line drawing, labels in a very small sans (8pt), center anchored by a 16pt typographic mark. Arrows as 0.3pt inward lines. Whole IPP spread spends 60% of its area on whitespace.

## Numbered markers

Small lining numerals in the margin (treat as side references), not inline. If the margin is too narrow, set them tight as superscripts at the start of the cited sentence.

## Ban list

- No serif typography
- No rules except structural (grid-defining) hairlines
- No boxes
- No more than one accent color
- No centered anything except on the cover
