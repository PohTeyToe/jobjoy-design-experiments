# Critic report — huashu v1

## Blocking defects
- [page 1] Cover wording does not match SAMPLE_1.md. Required literal copy is `JobJoy Report / For / Sample 1 / By: George Dutch, MA, CCDP / Date: April 24, 2026`. Variant instead displays the editorialized headline "A report on Sample 1, prepared in service of their right work." with a metadata strip (SUBJECT / COUNSELOR / METHOD / DATE). The required four-line block is absent. Semantic blocker.
- [page 4] Orphan/widow: the "How to read this report" body concludes with a single short word on its own last line ("it well.") before a paragraph gap, and the final paragraph ends with a two-word widow ("your Report."). Widows are called out explicitly in the blocking list.
- [pages 8–10] Three+ consecutive line-end hyphens. Page 8 has `imagina-/tion` and earlier `fin-/ished` within the same column near each other. Page 9 stacks `leads` region with `per-/former` and `cre-/ativity` and `pre-/determined` in close succession — multiple hyphenated line-ends within a short run. Page 10 shows `shel-/ters`, `profi-/ciency`, `high-/light`, `self-/contained` — four hyphenated line-ends in immediate sequence across the column. Violates the "3+ consecutive line-end hyphens" rule.
- [page 8] Straight apostrophe risk in PDF rendering: "didn't" (page 9) and "I've" render with ambiguous glyphs; at screenshot resolution the apostrophe in "didn't" (page 9, line 2 of paragraph 2) appears as a vertical straight tick rather than a curly glyph. If straight, this is a blocking typographic defect.
- [page 7] IPP arrow semantics partially broken. "01 END RESULT — Finished Product" sits above the center and its arrow points DOWN into the center (inward, OK), but the arrows from "07 Recognition Factor", "02 Subject Matter", "06 Natural Relating Style", "03 Talents — Doing", "05 Talents — Creating", and "04 Talents — Developing" all appear to originate from the center and extend outward toward the labels (the arrowheads sit at the label end, not the center end). Per brief, arrows must point INWARD toward Essential Motivation. Hard to confirm arrowhead direction at render resolution on several spokes, but at least the bottom-left and bottom-right spokes visibly have their arrowheads on the center side while upper satellites have heads on the outer side — arrow direction is inconsistent, which fails the IPP spec.

## Major defects
- [page 1] Measure violation on display type. The cover headline sets four lines of ~100pt display at a measure of roughly 12–14 characters per line — extreme, but it is display, not body, so not a body-CPL violation. However the metadata block at bottom-left sets its value column at ~30 characters — below the 60–68 CPL body floor. Since this is tabular meta it may be exempt, but it reads as body-weight 9–10pt and still sits outside measure.
- [page 2] Two-column colophon has the left column set at a measure of roughly 38–42 characters per line ("This report is set to a 13-point baseline grid on US-Letter stock…"), well below the 60–68 CPL floor for body. The right-column colophon entries are similarly narrow (~50 CPL). Measure violation across the entire page.
- [page 5] Chapter-opener display "Elements of your Key Success Factors." breaks "Key / Success Factors" across two lines with "Key" stranded at the end of line 1 — a runt/widow on a display head. The trailing period after "Factors" also feels heavy at display scale.
- [page 6] Body page is severely underfilled. Only six short definition paragraphs occupy roughly 40% of the text block; huge white band below. Body type color on this page is far below the 28–32% window — effectively <20% because so little ink lands on the page. Fails body type-color target.
- [page 7] "ESSENTIAL MOTIVATION" label is overlapped/crossed by the vertical arrow from "01 END RESULT". The arrow shaft passes through the final letter "N" of MOTIVATION. Typographic collision.
- [page 7] IPP label "07 RECOGNITION FACTOR" wraps "FACTOR" to a second line at tiny caps size, while the symmetric "02 SUBJECT MATTER" sits on one line. Asymmetric label wrapping breaks the geometric rigor the variant claims.
- [pages 8–10] Body measure on Personal Statement runs ~55–58 characters per line — below the 60–68 CPL floor. Consistent across all three continuation pages.
- [page 5] Heading ratio check: display "Elements of your Key Success Factors." at ~72pt vs. B-head "SIX ELEMENTS · ONE PATTERN" at ~11pt is a ratio of ~6.5 — not 1.333 between A/B/C heads. Heading scale is not on a perfect fourth.
- [page 8] Drop-cap / opening-indent alignment: paragraph one starts with no indent, paragraphs two through five start with a first-line indent of roughly 20pt. The first paragraph's flush-left start does not align with subsequent indented starts — indent protocol is inconsistent.
- [page 2] Running head reads "JOBJOY REPORT · COLOPHON" on left page but "SMYTH" alone on right — asymmetric running-head slotting contradicts brief requirement to "be consistent".

## Minor defects
- [page 1] Folio reads "01" but the footer also says "ONE OF ONE" — misleading on a 10-page document.
- [page 1] "SMYTH" as running footer on the cover is unusual; Sample 1 convention places Smyth on right-page footer only. Cover should not carry it.
- [page 3] TOC lists only 4 numbered sections plus 3 front-matter i/ii/iii — but document is 10 pages. Personal Statement continuation pages (09, 10) are not enumerated in TOC; only page 07 is cited for Personal Statement.
- [page 3] TOC leader dots are absent; dot leaders are the canonical TOC move on "the hardest typographic page in any book." Variant uses horizontal rules between entries instead — defensible but less disciplined.
- [pages 2, 7] Mixed numeral style: folio "02", "03" etc. use lining figures with letter-spacing; colophon metadata "9.5 / 13" uses lining; but body reads oldstyle-like in places. Brief requires one style held consistently for body vs. tabular — acceptable if intentional but the difference between folio spacing "0 2" vs. "02" across pages is uneven.
- [page 7] "Finished Product" label under "01 END RESULT" is set in black body weight while all other satellite values (e.g., "Influencer · Motivator · Solo") are also black — but value strings like "Sensory: Visual · People: Groups…" wrap to four lines, producing a bottom-heavy satellite. Layout imbalance.
- [pages 8–10] Numbered markers are superscript oxblood digits floating above baseline. On page 8 marker "1" after "creative project." sits too high and too far left, visually colliding with descenders above. Marker placement is not locked to the baseline grid.
- [page 9] "didn't" renders with the apostrophe offset to the right of the "n" by what looks like a full glyph width — kerning issue on "n't".
- [page 10] Orphan: paragraph beginning "I'm at another crossroads…" ends with "a creative project." as a two-word widow on its own line.

## Summary
5 blocking, 10 major, 8 minor. Verdict: needs revision.
