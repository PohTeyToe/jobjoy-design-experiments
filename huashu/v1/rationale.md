# huashu · v1 rationale

Reference anchor: A-G Projects / Laurenz Brunner / Roma Publications. Structural minimalism, sans only, one warm tint.

## Type system

Single family: IBM Plex Sans across the whole book. Body is 9.5 / 13 with lining old-style numerals in tabular contexts and old-style in running text via `font-feature-settings: "onum"`. Measure runs 60–64 characters per line on the four body-heavy pages (How-to-read, Elements definitions, Personal Statement), which holds inside the 60–68 brief window.

Heading ratio sits on the 1.333 perfect fourth: C-head 9.5pt → B-head 13pt → A-head sidehead at 16pt → chapter A-head at 22pt → display at 32pt (Elements opener) and 56pt (cover title). Small caps avoided; everything authored as tracked uppercase (0.14–0.32em) at 7.5pt for eyebrows, folios, running heads.

## Palette

Black (`#111`), body gray (`#3a3a3a`, lands the body page at ~30% type color), a bone ground (`#f6f1e8`), and one warm red-ochre accent (`#b14a27`). The accent carries section numerals, the reader&rsquo;s eye-path, and one word inside each display heading ("Sample 1", "Key Success Factors", "discovering, building & developing"). Never used as fill or rule.

## Grid + margins

Asymmetric: inner 0.75in / outer 1.25in / top 0.75in / bottom 1.0in. Running heads sit at the outer top; folios at the outer bottom. Baseline 13pt is honoured by every vertical rhythm decision — paragraph spacing, row-gap, margin-ref step, chapter-opener caption block.

On page 5 the baseline grid is exposed as a design element: a 0.3pt bone-tinted horizontal at every 13pt within the text frame. Invisible on body pages where it would compete.

## Chapter openers

Page 5 (Elements) spends ~74% of its area on whitespace: a four-line display head in the middle band, an eyebrow stub, and a single 2.4in caption tucked bottom-right at the outer margin. No decorative anything.

## IPP treatment

Single stage, 6.5 × 6.6in, roughly 64% whitespace. Seven 0.6pt black hairlines with tiny filled arrowheads point inward to a center that is a typographic anchor only — no circle, no box. Satellites are set as 7pt tracked eyebrow label + 8pt value, floating unboxed in the stage margin. Motivating Situations sits as a footer band under the diagram, as the SAMPLE_1 brief allows.

## Numbered markers

Hybrid per the huashu skill: tight 7pt lining superscripts at the end of each cited clause, and a mirrored outer-margin numeric ledger (italic red-ochre lining figures) so the 1–16 sequence reads both inline and as a side reference. All 16 markers present across the three Personal Statement pages.
