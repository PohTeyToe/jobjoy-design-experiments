# Worst remaining flaw

The IPP diagram (p7) is now symmetric and readable, but moving from 7 satellites to 6 meant dropping one of George's elements. The 7 Key Success Factor slots in the IPP are semantically distinct — collapsing to 6 is a design compromise that hides a methodological element rather than presenting it. A future pass should re-introduce the 7th satellite with a non-collinear geometry (e.g. a 7-point polygon with the "End Result" element seated on a stepped offset), keeping baseline symmetry without losing semantic completeness.
