## Blocking defects

## Major defects
- [page 6] IPP small-multiples grid uses a single bullet "•" as the only indicator of the central Essential Motivation tile — the caption admits "the grid itself is the diagram," but the central cell still reads as the weakest cell on the page rather than the gravitational center. Evidence: the Essential Motivation cell (center of the 3×3) contains less ink than any of its 8 neighbors, inverting the intended visual hierarchy.
- [page 4] "How to read this report" body column is noticeably narrow and the text is typeset justified with tight word-spacing, producing visible rivers in at least three paragraphs (lines beginning "Your JobJoy Report..." and "On reading your report..."). Measure appears below 60 CPL, outside the 60–68 spec.
- [page 5] Chapter opener display ("Elements of Your / Key Success Factors") is set around 42–48pt, not the 100–140pt called for in the brief's chapter-opener spec. The ratio between this display and the numbered C-heads is well below the 1.333 scale target — it is much larger.
- [page 2] Colophon block has a large empty upper half; the vertical composition is bottom-loaded with no deliberate anchor at top. For a title/colophon verso this reads as unfilled rather than composed negative space.

## Minor defects
- [page 1] Cover sets "JobJoy / Report" broken awkwardly across two lines with "Report" on its own line; the line break after "JobJoy" creates an unbalanced two-line display where a single-line or tighter stack would read stronger.
- [page 3] Contents page uses Roman numerals (I, II, III, IV) for sections but Arabic for page folios (1, 3, 5, 7) — acceptable convention, but the Roman "II" appears slightly lower-weight/smaller than "III"/"IV", suggesting inconsistent rendering or a kerning artifact.
- [page 4] Running foot reads "SMYTH   ELEMENTS" but this page is "How to Read This Report", not Elements — the running head appears to anticipate the next section rather than name the current one.
- [page 7] Personal Statement opener — the marginalia markers (1–5) are set quite close to the text block; optical spacing between measure and margin column could be wider to reinforce the Tufte sidenote idiom.
- [page 6] Column gutters inside the 3×3 grid are uneven vs. the surrounding page margins; the grid sits slightly left-of-center relative to the text frame above it.
- [page 8–9] Marginalia numerals 6–16 all appear; ordering and alignment hold. Minor: marker 10 ("Working through a group") wraps onto two lines while marker 9 ("Performer, influencer, motivator") wraps onto three — irregular marginalia block heights.
- [page 4] The em-rule under "How to read this report" italic head is a short single rule; consistent across the document, but on this page it sits unusually close to the heading baseline.

0 blocking, 4 major, 7 minor. Verdict: pass.
