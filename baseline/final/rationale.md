# Baseline v2 — rationale

Revision addresses the five blocking defects from the v1 critic pass and the follow-on majors.

**Chapter opener.** The 62-pt display head that orphaned "your" on its own line is replaced with a 34/40-pt italic serif A-head set in two explicit `<span class="line">` blocks: "Elements of Your / Key Success Factors" — matching the TOC entry verbatim. Terminal period dropped. The head now shares the A-head convention (italic Source Serif 4 at 18.7 pt scale family, bumped one step to 34 pt for chapter rank) with "Contents", "How to read this report", "Individual Passion Pattern", and "Personal Statement". Pick-and-hold across A-heads, satisfied.

**IPP.** The `&` in the center cell becomes ", and" — the document now reads "discovering, building, and developing" everywhere. The ambiguous "→" glyph on every satellite is replaced by a single Unicode geometric indicator whose direction is dictated by the cell's grid position: top row carries ▾ on the bottom edge, bottom row ▴ on the top edge, middle-left ▸ on the right edge, middle-right ◂ on the left edge. Every indicator points at the central Essential Motivation cell. Grid geometry remains the defensible small-multiples treatment the reference doc permits.

**Drop caps removed.** Both the PS opener and the "How to read this report" opener lose their drop cap. Tufte's marginalia books (VDQI, EI) set chapter openings flush with the body grid — a decorative initial is out of register for this posture. Removing the cap also removes the misaligned-shoulder defect.

**Apostrophes.** Every straight `'` in PS body copy is now `&rsquo;` (U+2019). `Kenex&rsquo;s`, `I&rsquo;ve`, `didn&rsquo;t`, `wasn&rsquo;t`, `it&rsquo;s`, `we&rsquo;d` all curled. Cover, colophon, TOC already clean.

**Marker completeness.** PS extends to a three-page spread (pages 7/8/9). Markers 1–5 on p. 7, 6–10 on p. 8, 11–16 on p. 9. All sixteen present.

**Numerals.** Old-style Source Serif 4 figures everywhere — TOC folios, element numerals, PS marginalia numerals, IPP labels — one style held across the book. The two-digit zero-pad ("01"–"06") is gone.

**Cover.** "JobJoy" and "Report" now stack as two roman lines in the same face and 40 pt size, separated by leading and a 0.5 pt hairline rule beneath. No italic on one word of a two-word title.

**Elements measure.** Six definitions drop from a two-column 40-CPL grid to a single 4.8-in column running ~62 CPL — inside the 60–68 band.

Measure: 4.3 in / 10.5 pt Source Serif 4 → 62 CPL. Baseline 13 pt held across body, marginalia offsets in 13 pt multiples. Heading ratio 10.5 → 13 → 18.7 → 34 sits at 1.333 between B-head, A-head, and chapter rank.
